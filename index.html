01 — About

Curious by default, practical by design.

Every project starts with the process behind it: where time is lost, which steps repeat and what can safely run on its own. I map the workflow, plan for the edge cases and agree on what success looks like before I build anything.

Curiosity keeps me testing new tools and models like Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, but each one has to earn its place in the stack. What I deliver are automations and AI agents that plug into the tools you already use, behave predictably and stay easy for your team to maintain.

03 — Process

How I work.

From the first call to a workflow your team can run on its own.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We talk through the process you want to automate, the apps involved and what a good result looks like.

  2. 02

    Propose

    I map the workflow in a clear proposal. Once you approve it, I build a working demo for you to try.

  3. 03

    Build & test

    I build it in n8n in my own environment first, then test it in the cloud before anything goes live.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    I walk you through setup, everyday use and error handling, so the workflow keeps running after handover.
