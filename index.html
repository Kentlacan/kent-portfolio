03 — Process

How I work.

From the first call to a workflow your team can run on its own.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We talk through the process you want to automate, the apps involved and what a good result looks like.

  2. 02

    Propose

    I map the workflow in a clear proposal. Once you approve it, I build a working demo for you to try.

  3. 03

    Build & test

    I build it in n8n in my own environment first, then test it in the cloud before anything goes live.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    I walk you through setup, everyday use and error handling, so the workflow keeps running after handover.
